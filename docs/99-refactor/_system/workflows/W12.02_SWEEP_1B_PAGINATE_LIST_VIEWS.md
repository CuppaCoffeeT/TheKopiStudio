## Why this exists

Split off W12 when the mechanical Sweep 1 closed. These 29 call sites are currently relying on PostgREST's silent 1,000-row cap — works today because our tables are small, breaks silently when they grow past 1k. Fix is NOT a one-liner: each needs a real pagination component wired through the URL (`?page=`, `?sort=`, `?search=`). Reference implementation: [PeopleManagement.tsx](../../../../src/pages/PeopleManagement.tsx) + [peopleService.ts](../../../../src/services/peopleService.ts) + [useURLPagination](../../../../src/hooks/useURLPagination.ts).
